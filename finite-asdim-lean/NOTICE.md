# Sources and attribution

## Mathematical source

Ruijun Wang, *Finite Borel asymptotic dimension of bounded-to-one commutative monoid actions*, **arXiv:2609.17177v1**, submitted 15 September 2026.

[Versioned abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17177v1) · [Versioned HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.17177v1)

The repository formalizes the ordinary asymptotic dimension assertion of Theorem 1.1 and its supporting proof. The arXiv version lists the paper under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The paper's Borel and hyperfiniteness corollaries are not formalized here.
